# Vayves development plan

Goal: Vayves (vayves.com, formerly stay by aMaldives) earns money when Maldives guesthouse and small-hotel owners claim their property, finish setup inside the 30-day trial and stay on a paid plan ($19 Growth, $49 Business, $79 Channel Plus a month, $29 Web add-on), plus a platform fee on bookings taken through it (4% direct, 10% via amaldives.com).

Where it stands on 2026-11-24: the product is broad (PMS, booking engine, iCal and OTA-email sync, MIRA tax, BML/Maya/Stripe payments, agents, promotions, guest chat) and Stripe subscription checkout with a 30-day trial is wired. Stripe shows no paid subscription in the last 30 days. Search Console (2026-11-21) reports every marketing page as "Discovered - currently not indexed". Booking platform fees are calculated and stored but nothing collects them. No funnel events are sent to GA4. See `docs/seo-approach.md` and `docs/VAYVES-SEO-ROADMAP.md` for the search plan this builds on.
