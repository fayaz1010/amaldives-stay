# Vayves SEO Roadmap — cold-start build order + briefs

Generated 2026-07-07 via the seo-strategy flywheel. vayves.com = brand-new domain (zero GSC
history), rebranded from stay.amaldives.com. This is the **build order + brief sheet**; ship
pages, then measure in GSC after 4–8 weeks.

## Phase 0 — Archetype & wedge
- **Archetype:** Commercial-intent SaaS (few pages, high-ticket B2B). Lever = **buyer-intent
  vertical landing pages**, NOT article-gen.
- **Wedge (must appear above the fold on every page):** *"The hotel PMS with the Maldives tax
  engine built in (TGST · Green Tax · MIRA 206) — and free guest demand from amaldives.com.
  Commission-free direct bookings, not just software."*
- **Real expertise (E-E-A-T is genuine, do not fabricate):** live Maldives properties on the
  system (Rivethi Beach, H78 group); a working MIRA/TGST/Green-Tax module; founder operating
  in-market; the amaldives.com distribution channel no pure-PMS competitor has.
- **Conversion mechanism:** "Claim your free account" / "Book a demo" — one primary CTA/page.

## SERP findings (live, 2026-07-07)
- **`hotel management software` (head) = HARD.** 4 paid ads (RateGain, Twilio, Agilysys,
  RoomPriceGenie), AI Overview, organic owned by Cloudbeds / HotelTechReport / Hotelogix
  "10 best" listicles + review directories. A new domain cannot win this → **parked**.
  But the AI Overview routes *"small properties, B&Bs & guesthouses ≤50 rooms"* to Little
  Hotelier/eviivo — that niche is the opening.
- **`maldives guesthouse management software` = MEDIUM (winnable).** Local incumbents
  (Tharazoo, fahiHMS/Terabyte, Fenuva PMS, Cloud Guest House/Strings, eZee via Acuity,
  Hotelogix via Octopus) — beatable with real MIRA-tax depth + zero-commission + amaldives
  distribution. AI Overview frames the need as "TGST, Green Tax, MIRA 206" = our exact wedge.
